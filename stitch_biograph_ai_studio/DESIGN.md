## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a synthesis of high-end scientific precision and haute horlogerie editorial luxury. Tailored for computational biochemists, principal investigators, and medicinal chemists conducting molecular property prediction, the interface avoids the cluttered, hyper-dense tropes of legacy laboratory software. Instead, it balances the clinical discipline of Linear with the dimensional tactility of visionOS and the understated opulence of an archival catalog.

The visual ethos relies on quiet confidence, deliberate pacing, and deep atmospheric depth. Every interaction should evoke the tactile precision of handling bespoke scientific instrumentation: perfectly weighted, visually silent until engaged, and rigorously legible. High molecular complexity is tamed through expansive negative space, sculptural translucent planes, and bespoke jewel-toned accents that serve immediate analytical utility.

## Colors

The palette is engineered exclusively for an immersive, reduced-fatigue dark environment built from organic carbon and mineral tones.

### Canvas & Base Surfaces
- **Obsidian Canvas (`#08090B`)**: Deep void base layer, calibrated with a soft radial bloom centered in the upper third shifting imperceptibly toward warm graphite (`#15181D`).
- **Graphite Base Surface (`#111317`)**: Foundational structural container tone.
- **Smoke Surface (`#1A1D22`)**: Elevated interactive surface for nested structures and active states.

### Typography & Content
- **Ivory (`#F4F1EA`)**: Primary typographic color, warm white with zero blue light glare.
- **Warm Stone (`#A39F96`)**: Secondary metadata, labels, and muted structural cues.
- **Basalt Tertiary (`#6E6B65`)**: Inactive states, subtle dividers, and tertiary chemical descriptors.

### Signature Accent
- **Champagne Gold (`#D4B483`)**: Primary interactive accent, molecular selection targets, and validated endpoints.
- **Champagne Gold Pressed (`#B8925A`)**: Active depressed state, providing mechanical warmth.

### Semantic Tones
- **Success (Sage)**: `#7FB89A`
- **Caution (Amber)**: `#D9A25B`
- **Danger (Garnet)**: `#C65B5B`
- **Info (Slate Blue)**: `#7C9CC4`

### Architectural Model Palette
Dedicated exclusively to distinguish machine learning architectures in predictive overlays:
- **MPNN (Amethyst)**: `#9A86C9`
- **GIN (Rose Quartz)**: `#C98AA3`
- **GAT (Copper)**: `#C98B5E`
- **GCN (Steel Blue)**: `#7C9CC4`
- **Random Forest (Sage)**: `#7FB89A`

## Typography

The typographical landscape pairs editorial serif warmth with modern hyper-rational sans and clinical monospaced logic.

- **Newsreader** delivers authoritative weight for hero affinity scores, binding constants, and editorial page introductions. It is strictly rendered with optical sizing and delicate ligature support.
- **Geist** governs interactive mechanics, forms, parameter configurations, and complex multi-column scientific layouts. It enforces neutral clarity without distracting decorative glyphs.
- **JetBrains Mono** is reserved for molecular notations: canonical SMILES, FASTA sequences, InChI keys, thermodynamic readouts, and tabular data tables.

All numerical readouts, confidence intervals, and metrics must employ tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time computational inference.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an 8pt architectural rhythm, biased toward generous negative space that honors high cognitive load tasks.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Desktop (1440px and above)**: 12-column fluid grid with 32px gutters and 48px outer canvas margins. Scientific workspaces feature a primary dock on the left, an infinite canvas for 3D molecular conformations, and an inspector rail (fixed 420px width) on the right.
- **Tablet / Workstation Small (1024px to 1439px)**: 8-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Inspector rails collapse into slide-over frosted panels.
- **Mobile (below 1024px)**: Single-column linear flow with 16px gutters and 20px edge margins. Visual 3D viewports fix aspect ratio (1:1 or 4:3), docking prediction cards beneath.

Component padding operates between 28px and 40px on primary containers, ensuring cards feel spacious and exhibition-grade rather than compressed.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through optical transmission and physical glass optics rather than stark drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Glass Layer
Panels occupy physical space above the dark carbon void:
- **Surface Fill**: Translucent white fill between `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Blur**: `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(140%)`.
- **Outer Perimeter**: 1px directional border gradient running from top-left (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`) to bottom-right (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`).
- **Inner Rim**: Hairline inner specular highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Ambient Occlusion**: Deep, dispersed shadow (`0 24px 60px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`).

### Elevation Tiers
1. **Tier 0 (Substrate)**: Obsidian background (`#08090B`) with central warm graphite luminescence (`#15181D`).
2. **Tier 1 (Base Cards & Viewports)**: Solid Graphite `#111317` with subtle 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
3. **Tier 2 (Floating Glass Panels & Modals)**: Standard Atmospheric Glass Layer above Tier 1 or the 3D molecular canvas.
4. **Tier 3 (Contextual HUDs & Popovers)**: Elevated glass with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` fill, and sharp dual-stage rim illumination.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects machined precision with ergonomic curvature.

- **Primary Cards and Panels**: Fixed at `24px` corner radii, creating an organic viewport reminiscent of high-end hardware enclosures.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Segmented Selectors)**: Calibrated at `14px` corner radii, maintaining distinct visual continuity with outer cards.
- **Pills, Badges, and Chemical Tags**: Fully circular radii (`9999px`) to emphasize discrete molecular identities and categorizations.
- **Sub-nested Containers**: 12px corner radii to establish a strict 2:1 geometric nesting ratio within 24px parent panels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Champagne Gold (`#D4B483`), text in Obsidian `#08090B` (`font-weight: 500`). Hover elevates brightness by 6%; active transition settles to `#B8925A`. Radius is 14px.
- **Secondary (Glass)**: Atmospheric Glass substrate, text in Ivory (`#F4F1EA`), with subtle 1px border. Hover transitions to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost**: Text in Warm Stone (`#A39F96`), zero background, hovering to Ivory text with soft smoke surface bloom.

### Form Inputs & Chemical Entry
- **Height**: 48px baseline, 14px radius.
- **Base State**: Background in `#111317`, 1px perimeter border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Placeholder text in Basalt Tertiary (`#6E6B65`).
- **Focus State**: Hairline border transitions to Champagne Gold (`rgba(212, 180, 131, 0.8)`), accompanied by a diffused outer glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 180, 131, 0.15)`). Monospaced styling is enforced for SMILES inputs.

### Chips & Model Badges
- Pill-shaped (`9999px`), 28px height, padded with 12px horizontal margins.
- Neutral states use low-opacity Smoke with Warm Stone typography.
- Machine learning model indicators use their designated architectural hue at 12% fill opacity, paired with an inner border of 24% opacity and solid jewel text (e.g., MPNN Amethyst `#9A86C9`).

### Scientific Metric Cards
- 24px border radius, frosted glass backdrop, generous 32px inner padding.
- Metric values (pIC50, logP, molecular weight) display prominently in Newsreader display typography with JetBrains Mono unit suffixes.
- Confidence intervals are visualized via a 4px horizontal micro-gauge utilizing the semantic color spectrum (Sage to Garnet).

### Molecular Structure Viewports
- Edge-to-edge Obsidian surface with radial graphite glow.
- Controls float in an Atmospheric Glass toolbar positioned at the lower center with 14px radius buttons and 16px gaps.
